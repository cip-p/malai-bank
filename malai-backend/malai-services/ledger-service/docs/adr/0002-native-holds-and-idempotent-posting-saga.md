# In-flight money is a native ledger Hold; cross-service consistency is an idempotent-posting saga

`ledger-service` models money in-flight as a first-class **Hold** — a reservation that reduces an account's **Available balance** without moving its **Ledger balance** — rather than as the `customer → suspense` double-entry posting `payments-service` used before. A **Hold** is `PLACED` then either **settled** (atomic post-and-release: the hold is marked `SETTLED` and a balanced **Posting** debiting the account is recorded in one ledger transaction) or **released** (cancelled, posting nothing). Because the ledger now lives behind an HTTP boundary, the single shared DB transaction that previously made the payment row and its ledger entries atomic is gone; consistency is instead an **idempotent-posting saga** — every **Posting**/**Hold** is idempotent on a caller-supplied id, the funds guard is enforced atomically inside the ledger on **Available balance**, and the `payments` row is the saga coordinator that drives retries. This **supersedes the suspense-account model** for the outbound side described in `payments-service` ADR-0001/0002.

## Considered options

- **Posting-only; keep suspense accounts** *(rejected).* A behaviour-preserving extraction: the ledger exposes only the balanced **Posting**, and `payments-service` keeps orchestrating `customer → suspense` postings over the new API. Minimal and lowest-risk — but it leaves "block/settle" smeared across payments + suspense accounts, keeps available-balance a recomputed `payments-service` concern, and is less like the Mambu seam this extraction is built around. The brief explicitly wants block/settle as a demonstrable ledger capability.
- **Native holds; re-model FPS outbound** *(accepted).* The Mambu-shaped primitive. Makes **Available balance** a real ledger projection (`Ledger balance − Held amount`) instead of something payments recomputes from `PENDING` rows, and *deletes* machinery: the outbound suspense accounts disappear, and a `RETURNED` outbound payment posts **nothing** (its hold is released, never settled). Cost is real — it is a behaviour change, not a behaviour-preserving move.
- **Best-effort synchronous consistency (5xx the request on any ledger failure, accept the inconsistency window)** *(rejected).* Least machinery, fine for a pure demo — but it does not meet the "heart and core, has to work well" bar and is not how a real ledger integration behaves.

## Decisions

- **Funds guard is the ledger's invariant, on Available balance.** A **Posting** or **Hold** that would take a guarded account's **Available balance** below its floor is rejected atomically (account locked, available checked, applied-or-rejected in one transaction). Callers stop pre-checking and handle a `LEDGER_INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` rejection. This is exactly how Mambu rejects a breaching posting.
- **Idempotency, not distributed transactions.** Replaying a posting/hold id is a no-op returning the original result, so at-least-once HTTP delivery yields exactly-once money movement. The `payments` row stays the lifecycle authority (root ADR-0002); the ledger is the money authority; they reconcile by the shared id. A reconciliation sweep advances stuck `PENDING` payments.
- **Hold scope is minimal.** States `PLACED → SETTLED | RELEASED` only (no expiry). A hold carries only `(accountId, amount)`; the destination is supplied at settle. Settle is **full-amount only** (no partial capture). All three are real ledger features deliberately deferred as unused by any malai rail in v1.

## Consequences

- **Outbound** suspense accounts (`FPS_OUTBOUND_SUSPENSE`, `SWIFT_OUTBOUND_SUSPENSE`) are retired; **inbound** suspense + `SCHEME_SETTLEMENT` survive as `ledger-service` **System account**s (inbound is still a straight credit posting). On settle, an outbound payment posts `customer → SCHEME_SETTLEMENT` directly.
- `payments-service`'s `transitionToReturned` loses its entire reversal-posting block (a return is now just `releaseHold`). `PaymentsService`/`PaymentLifecycleService` stop importing `account-service` ledger entities and call the ledger client instead.
- **Statements/transaction-list show only posted money.** A held-but-unsettled payment has no ledger entry and does not appear until it settles; a returned payment never appears. A "pending" line, if the webapp wants one, comes from `payments-service` `PENDING` rows, not the ledger.
- The `payments-service` glossary's `Available balance` / `Settled balance` terms are retired in favour of the ledger's **Ledger balance** / **Held amount** / **Available balance**; the webapp must lead with **Available**, since **Ledger balance** no longer drops at `PENDING`.
- Supersedes the outbound suspense-account mechanism in `payments-service` ADR-0001 and ADR-0002.
