# 07 — A seeded Banking day that reconciles across Surfaces

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement

## What to build

The largest single item in `.scratch/exec-metrics/PRD.md`, and independent of every metrics slice — it can start immediately and run in parallel.

A freshly reset demo environment currently produces an Exec dashboard of near-zeroes, and a worklist whose seven seeded **Decision**s are correlated to `CARD_AUTHORISATION` and `ACCOUNT` — not one to a `PAYMENT`, and no seeded payment has ever been declined. So the Payments-stopped tile would read zero next to a busy Ops worklist: two Surfaces describing the same bank and disagreeing. The evaluator this product is built for is precisely the person who clicks the number to see what is behind it.

Seed a plausible **Banking day** whose figures reconcile when drilled into. The core of it is the screening story, seeded end to end rather than faked at either end: a few outbound payments referred with real `screening_decision_id` pointers on the payment rows, their `MODEL_DECISION` entries appended under `malai-screening-rules` version `1` with `PAYMENT` correlation refs and genuine prompt-template pins, adverse **Human Disposition**s authored by the seeded ops reviewer, and the resulting payments moved to `DECLINED`. The Payments-stopped figure and the Ops worklist then become the same facts read two ways.

Alongside it, enough new customers, settled inbound payments, and issued cards that the other three tiles read like a small bank's morning rather than a test fixture. Judgement call on volumes — plausible beats impressive; a number that looks fabricated is worse than a modest one.

Entries append through the service layer with fixed entry ids so replay stays idempotent, per the existing MDR seeding pattern. The existing `MdrDemoSeed` entries stay exactly as they are — a mixed-model worklist was already accepted as realistic, and a bank running several deciders is truthful.

Seeded timestamps are already now-relative and `ResetCommand` re-anchors the estate to run time, so the operational rule is unchanged: reset before demoing. Nothing here should introduce absolute dates that rot.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fresh reset produces a non-zero, plausible figure for all four metrics
- [ ] Seeded referred payments carry real `screening_decision_id` pointers to seeded Model Decisions with `PAYMENT` correlation refs
- [ ] Seeded Model Decisions for screening are recorded under `malai-screening-rules` version `1`, with a genuine prompt-template pin — never a fictional model for a payment that really was screened
- [ ] Seeded adverse dispositions are authored by the seeded ops reviewer and move their payments to `DECLINED`
- [ ] The Payments-stopped figure equals the sum of the seeded declined payments — the number and the worklist reconcile
- [ ] Seeded declined payments hold no ledger entries and no active Hold, per the lifecycle invariants
- [ ] Existing `MdrDemoSeed` entries are unchanged
- [ ] Seeding is idempotent, clean removes the additions surgically, and all timestamps are relative to run time
- [ ] Chain verification walks the seeded entries and reports intact
- [ ] `mvn install` green
