# 03 — The Cards issued metric

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement

## What to build

Third slice of `.scratch/exec-metrics/PRD.md`. The **Cards issued** tile stops reading `UNAVAILABLE` and starts reporting the day's issuance.

`card-service` gains a service-JWT-scoped internal metric read counting **Card**s issued during the **Banking day**; `exec-service` adds the fan-out leg with the established timeout and `UNAVAILABLE` degradation.

The definition that matters: this counts **issuance events, not surviving cards**. A card issued this morning and cancelled this afternoon was still issued today, and the figure must not decrease when it is cancelled — no bank's "cards issued today" silently goes down. Both variants count; a replacement card issued after a loss is an issuance like any other.

No contract change — the tracer already published the response shape.

## Acceptance criteria
