# 04 — The Payments stopped metric

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement

## What to build

Fourth slice of `.scratch/exec-metrics/PRD.md`, and the tile that closes the enforcement loop onto the executive view. **Payments stopped** sums the **Payment**s the bank refused after **Screening** review during the **Banking day**.

`payments-service` gains a service-JWT-scoped internal metric read summing payments in the `DECLINED` **Lifecycle** state; `exec-service` adds the fan-out leg with the established timeout and `UNAVAILABLE` degradation.

**Source it from the payment state, not from the MDR.** `DECLINED` is set in exactly one place — the resolver enacting an actionable `DECLINE` — so the state *is* the enacted result of a human ruling, already persisted. Re-deriving the figure from Human Dispositions would reimplement the accept/edit/reject → actionable-output translation that ADR-0009 keeps in exactly one place, and would miss an *edit* to a DECLINE verdict. Both the client brief ("MDR reject rulings") and the payment-screening PRD ("accept-DECLINE rulings") specify this wrongly; a *reject* ruling leaves a payment parked and stops nothing. The link to the audit plane survives via `screening_decision_id` on the payment row for anyone drilling through.

The tile is **not** called "fraud prevented" anywhere — copy, field names, or contract description. The decider is a deterministic rules engine, and a declined payment is money the bank stopped, not proven fraud. Carries an explicit currency, GBP only, non-GBP excluded and stated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `GET /api/v1/exec/metrics` returns Payments stopped as a real GBP figure for the current Banking day
- [ ] The figure is derived from the `DECLINED` payment state, not from MDR entries
- [ ] A payment declined via an *edit*-to-DECLINE ruling counts, exactly as an accepted DECLINE does
- [ ] A payment still parked in `REFERRED` (including one whose latest ruling is *reject*) contributes nothing
- [ ] A non-GBP declined payment is excluded, and the exclusion is stated in the contract
- [ ] A payment declined either side of the `Europe/London` midnight boundary falls on the correct day
- [ ] An unreachable or slow `payments-service` degrades Payments stopped alone to `UNAVAILABLE`
- [ ] Nothing in the contract, field names, or descriptions uses "fraud prevented"
- [ ] The internal metric read is service-JWT-scoped and stays out of the public contract
- [ ] `mvn install` green
