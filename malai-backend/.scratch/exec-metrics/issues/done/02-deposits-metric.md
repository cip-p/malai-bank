# 02 — The Deposits metric

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement

## What to build

Second slice of `.scratch/exec-metrics/PRD.md`. The **Deposits** tile stops reading `UNAVAILABLE` and starts reporting money that entered the bank from outside during the **Banking day**.

`payments-service` gains a service-JWT-scoped internal metric read summing inbound **Payment**s that settled during the Banking day; `exec-service` adds the fan-out leg, with the same per-source timeout and `UNAVAILABLE` degradation the tracer established.

The exclusions are the point of this slice and are what the tests must pin. A **Book transfer** contributes nothing — both legs are internal, and counting them would let a customer inflate the bank's headline figure by moving their own money between their own accounts. A card refund contributes nothing: it reverses spend, it is not a deposit. Anything not yet settled contributes nothing. It is a flow figure for the day, so a payment that settles today and is returned tomorrow stays counted.

The metric carries an explicit currency and reports GBP only: non-GBP inbound payments are excluded from the figure and stated as excluded in the contract, never silently summed in. Minor units of different currencies are not addable, and a total that mixed them would be a real bug in front of exactly the audience most likely to spot it.

No contract change to the response shape — the tracer already published it. The `UNAVAILABLE` path stays reachable for when the source is unhealthy.

- [ ] A book transfer contributes nothing to the figure

- [ ] A non-GBP inbound payment is excluded, and the exclusion is stated in the contract rather than left implicit

### Resolution (2026-07-20)

Done. The Deposits tile reads a real GBP figure for the current Banking day.

- **Definition.** A Deposit is an `INBOUND`, `SETTLED` payment that credited a Malai account, GBP,
  in the half-open window. `payments-service` gained `GET /api/v1/internal/payments/deposits-total`
  (service-JWT-scoped, out of the public contract) summing exactly that, and `exec-service` added the
  fan-out leg via `DepositsSource` + `MalaiPaymentsMetricsClient`, degrading to `UNAVAILABLE` alone
  when payments-service is unreachable or slow.
- **The "credited a Malai account" predicate is what carries the exclusions.** A Book transfer is
  `OUTBOUND` (one row, from the initiator's side) so it never counts. A direct-debit *collection*
  debits the customer and credits no account — no beneficiary — so it drops out (this case is beyond
  the issue's stated list but is a real correctness case and is pinned by a repository test). Anything
  unsettled is excluded by state; non-GBP is excluded by currency.
- **Card refunds are excluded structurally, not by a filter.** A card refund never becomes a payment
  row — it is a `CARD_REFUND` ledger credit owned by card-service — so it cannot reach a sum sourced
  from inbound payment rows. Documented in the service KDoc and the exec `CONTEXT.md`; there is no
  payments-service test for it because there is nothing to insert (a card refund is not representable
  in the `payments` table).
- **No settlement timestamp needed.** Inbound credits are born `SETTLED` with `created_at` = the
  settlement moment, so the window keys on `created_at`. It is a flow figure: inbound credits never
  leave `SETTLED`, so a later state change cannot retroactively reduce it.
- **No contract change.** issue 01 already published `deposits`, `MoneyMetric`, and the GBP-only
  exclusion in `openapi.yaml`; `git diff` over the spec is 0 lines. Endpoint still gates on `ADMIN`
  (issue 05 widens to `MANAGER`).
